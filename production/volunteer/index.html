Day-of help

Help run Elk Grove Lunar New Year Tết at Elk Grove Park — open to adults and high school students. Pick a time shift on the form; at HQ check-in we assign stations. This is an interest sign-up; everyone is waitlisted until we approve.

Sat & Sun

Sat & Sun
5:00–9:00 PM		Evening / Strike — Info booth through last guests (~7 PM); then tear down tables, chairs, and décor for load-out. Sun strike is usually the heavier leave-behind.
