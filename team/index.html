Join the team

Show up to weekly production calls and Discord first. Program gift DRIs, Outreach Directors, and the Site & Ops Supercommittee are listed on their own page. We also welcome visionaries who want to express celebration through the festival journey.

What is a director?

A director (or DRI) is the accountable owner of one festival lane or Fund The Festival gift. You recommend and decide budget for your lane, including whether to contract out work. You recruit help, hire or place pros (paid or pro bono), and report status to the Steering Committee. You are not assumed to build sets, hang decor, or personally perform the craft.
