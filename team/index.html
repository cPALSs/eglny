Join the team

Show up to weekly production calls and Discord first. Traditional director lanes are still open — listed on their own page. We also welcome visionaries who want to express celebration through the festival journey.

What is a director?

A director is the go-to person for one festival lane. You don't have to do every task yourself — you own the plan, coordinate vendors and volunteers, and report status to the Steering Committee. Most roles are planning now through February 2027; peak workload is the two weeks before and event weekend.
