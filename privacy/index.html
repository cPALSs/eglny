Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Site: eglny.com (Elk Grove Lunar New Year Tết)
Operator: Community Partners Advocate of Little Saigon Sacramento (cPALSs), on behalf of the Elk Grove Lunar New Year Tết organizing coalition (cPALSs, Vietnamese American Community of Sacramento, and NorCal Vietnamese Chamber of Commerce)
Contact: contact@eglny.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you visit eglny.com. It applies to this website only.

eglny.com is an informational festival website. You do not create an account, sign in, or submit payment directly on this site. When you click through to apply as a vendor, volunteer, sponsor, or activity participant, you use separate platforms with their own privacy policies (see Section 4).

2. Information we collect

  • Analytics (Google Analytics): We use Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand aggregate site traffic — such as which pages are visited, general region, device/browser type, and referring links. Google Analytics may set cookies or use similar technologies in your browser.
  • Browser preferences: We store your light/dark theme preference in your browser’s sessionStorage so the site remembers your display choice while you browse. We do not use browser storage for advertising or cross-site tracking.
  • Hosting logs: This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard web server and network logs (such as IP address, browser user agent, requested page, and timestamp) to deliver the site and protect against abuse.
  • Email inquiries: If you email contact@eglny.com, sponsorship@eglny.com, or a listed program contact, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to share.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate and improve eglny.com, understand which festival guides (such as the food menu, vendor list, season calendar, and sponsorship pages) are useful to visitors, and respond to questions or partnership inquiries.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use site data for third-party advertising.

4. Third-party platforms and links

Several actions on eglny.com link to external platforms operated by third parties or by cPALSs. Their privacy policies apply when you use them:

  • Eventeny — vendor booth applications, selection, and booth payments
  • Bugle Volunteers — festival-weekend volunteer interest sign-ups
  • Idealist — planning Coordinator and volunteer leadership applications
  • Tally & Google Forms — activity registrations (such as Star of Spring Mini-Pageant) and stage speaking requests
  • cPALSs Portal (portal.cpalss.com) — sponsor and vendor hubs, club rosters, and waivers (see the cPALSs Portal Privacy Policy)
  • Social, video & community links — Instagram, Facebook, Discord, YouTube (embedded festival videos), and Google Drive (downloadable sponsorship packet)

5. Children and youth

eglny.com is a general-audience family festival site and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Youth volunteering, pageant entries, and school-club participation are handled through separate registration platforms with parent or guardian involvement where required.

6. Retention and security

Email correspondence is retained as needed for festival operations and nonprofit recordkeeping. Aggregate analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings. We use HTTPS and standard web hosting safeguards, though no internet transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your choices

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. California residents and other visitors may email contact@eglny.com to request access to or deletion of email correspondence we hold.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the festival site evolves. When we do, we will update the Effective date at the top of this page.
