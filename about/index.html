About Elk Grove Lunar New Year Tết

Tết

Lunar New Year, known as Tết in Vietnamese culture, is one of the most significant and cherished celebrations in Asian communities worldwide.

Strategic objectives

Six objectives guide how we plan the weekend: youth leadership, multi-generational families and seniors, Vietnamese American business, cultural preservation, cross-cultural welcome, and year-round partnership.

Lucky Wav

For 2027, Lucky Wav is the main-stage music journey at Elk Grove Park — Lucky for blessings ahead, Wav for music, movement, and a new wave of culture.
